Introduction / 引言
0.1 Background / 背景
A digital product passport (DPP) is a key enabling mechanism to make product information traceable and
accessible across value chains — supporting economic operators, manufacturers, distributors, repairers,
recyclers and consumers to make informed decisions and to support a circular economy. The
implementation of DPPs will be carried out progressively. Sector-specific initiatives will determine the
precise DPP content and requirements for individual product groups.
To support the implementation of DPPs, 8 standards have been developed:
— EN 18219:2026 – Digital product passport – Unique identifiers
— EN 18220:2026 – Digital product passport – Data carriers
— EN 18216:2026 – Digital product passport – Data exchange protocols
— EN 18222:2026 – Digital product passport – APIs for product passport lifecycle management and
searchability
— EN 18223:2026 – Digital product passport – System interoperability
— EN 18221:2026 – Digital product passport – Data storage, archiving, and data persistence
— EN 18239:2026 – Digital product passport – Access rights management, information system security,
and business confidentiality (this document)
— EN 18246:2026 – Digital product passport – Data authentication, reliability and integrity
  数字产品护照（DPP）是实现产品信息在整个价值链中可追溯和可访问的关键赋能机制——支持经济运营者、
  制造商、分销商、维修商、回收商和消费者做出明智决策，并支持循环经济。DPP 的实施将逐步推进。特定行
  业倡议将确定各产品组的具体 DPP 内容和要求。
  为支持 DPP 的实施，已制定 8 项标准：
  — EN 18219:2026 – 数字产品护照 – 唯一标识符
  — EN 18220:2026 – 数字产品护照 – 数据载体
  — EN 18216:2026 – 数字产品护照 – 数据交换协议
  — EN 18222:2026 – 数字产品护照 – 产品护照生命周期管理和可搜索性的 API
  — EN 18223:2026 – 数字产品护照 – 系统互操作性
  — EN 18221:2026 – 数字产品护照 – 数据存储、归档和数据持久性
  — EN 18239:2026 – 数字产品护照 – 访问权限管理、信息系统安全和商业保密（本文件）
  — EN 18246:2026 – 数字产品护照 – 数据认证、可靠性和完整性
0.2 Overview / 概述
This document aims at harmonizing the identity management that ensures that organisations, individuals,
machines and services are provided with acknowledged identities. This document defines clear
requirements related to access control measures to regulate the access to controlled DPP data.
This document defines rules and requirements related to:
— access right management;
— access control;
— clarification of DPP system roles and responsibilities to provide the means to define mechanisms on
how to exchange access right information between economic operators, back-up system operators and
registry of the regulated market controlling entity;
— measures to regulate the access to controlled DPP data;
— possibility for product group specific definition of access rights by relevant legal acts;
— information system security;
— business confidentiality and their representation in access rights management;
— differentiation of access rights of different user groups and authorities;
— guarantee of IT-security, cybersecurity, and data protection; and
— clarification of DPP system roles and responsibilities to provide the means to define mechanisms on
how to transfer responsibilities, access-rights, and data from one economic operator to another.
  EXAMPLE When a DPP will need to be updated to include information related to repair activities performed by a
  professional repairer.
  本文件旨在协调身份管理，确保组织、个人、机器和服务获得公认的身份。本文件定义了与访问控制措施相关
  的明确要求，以规范对受控 DPP 数据的访问。
  本文件定义了以下方面的规则和要求：
  — 访问权限管理；
  — 访问控制；
  — 明确 DPP 系统角色和职责，为定义经济运营者、备份系统运营商和受监管市场控制实体登记处之间交换访
  问权限信息的机制提供手段；
  — 规范受控 DPP 数据访问的措施；
  — 由相关法律行为针对特定产品组定义访问权限的可能性；
  — 信息系统安全；
  — 商业保密及其在访问权限管理中的表示；
  — 不同用户组和机构的访问权限区分；
  — 保证 IT 安全、网络安全和数据保护；以及
  — 明确 DPP 系统角色和职责，为定义将一个经济运营者的责任、访问权限和数据转移给另一个经济运营者的
  机制提供手段。
    示例：当 DPP 需要更新以包含专业维修商执行的维修活动相关信息时。

1 Scope / 范围
This document specifies the requirements for digital product passport (DPP) access rights management,
including IT security, data protection, and responsibility transfer between economic operators. It defines
the framework for managing confidential information access, while acknowledging that public DPP data
requires no access restrictions to be read.
  本文件规定了数字产品护照（DPP）访问权限管理的要求，包括 IT 安全、数据保护和经济运营者之间的责任转
  移。它定义了管理机密信息访问的框架，同时确认公共 DPP 数据的读取不需要访问限制。

4 Business transactions and responsibilities related to the DPP / DPP 相
关业务交易与责任
4.1 Business aspects of the DPP life cycle as basis of access management / DPP 生命周期的业
务方面作为访问管理的基础
Access rights management is defined for the different life cycle phases and stages of a DPP. Such phases
and stages are based on EN ISO 11354-1:2011.
In general, not all the phases and the stages mentioned in this document might be applicable to every
product sector. Therefore, only the phases and stages relevant for a specific product group shall be
considered. Moreover, in case a product group specific stage is not represented in the following list, it shall
be considered during the access rights management implementation.
  访问权限管理针对 DPP 的不同生命周期阶段和步骤进行定义。这些阶段和步骤基于 EN ISO 11354-1:2011。
  一般而言，本文件中提到的所有阶段和步骤可能并不适用于每个产品行业。因此，仅应考虑与特定产品组相关
  的阶段和步骤。此外，如果以下列表中未包含特定产品组的阶段，应在访问权限管理实施过程中予以考虑。
4.2 Actors along the DPP life cycle / DPP 生命周期中的主体
This section identifies the main actors who are responsible for the handling of a DPP relevant product
across the value chain and therefore need to access a DPP along its business stages. The identified actors
are the following:
1. Economic operators (in short EOs): role encompassing the manufacturer, the authorized
representative, the importer, the distributor, the dealer and the fulfilment service provider.
     a. Manufacturers: any natural or legal person that manufactures a product or that has a product
     designed or manufactured and markets that product under their name or trademark.
b. Authorized representatives: any natural or legal person established in the regulated market that
has received a legally binding (e.g. written) mandate from the manufacturer to act on the
manufacturer's behalf in relation to specified tasks with regard to the manufacturer's obligations.
c. Importers: any natural or legal person that places a product coming from outside the regulated
market on the regulated market.
d. Distributors: any natural or legal person in the supply chain, other than the manufacturer or the
importer, that makes a product available on the market.
e. Dealers: a distributor or any other natural or legal person that offers products for sale, hire or hire
purchase, or that displays products to end users in the course of a commercial activity, including
through distance selling; and includes any natural or legal person that puts a product into service in
the course of a commercial activity.
f. Fulfilment service providers: any natural or legal person offering, in the course of commercial
activity, services such as: warehousing, packaging, addressing and dispatching, without having
ownership of the products involved.

4.2 Actors along the DPP life cycle (continued) / DPP 生命周期中的主体（续）
2. Other value chain actors
     a. Consumers: individual member of the general public purchasing or using goods, property or
     services for private purposes [EN ISO 14025:2010, 3.16].
     b. Professional repairers: a natural or legal person that provides professional repair or maintenance
     services for a product, irrespective of whether that person acts within the manufacturer's distribution

     c. Independent operators: natural or legal person that is independent of the manufacturer and is
     directly or indirectly involved in the refurbishment, repair, maintenance or repurposing of a product,
     and includes waste management operators, refurbishers, repairers, manufacturers or distributors of
     repair equipment, tools or spare parts, as well as publishers of technical information, operators

     and repairers of equipment.
     d. Recycler: any natural or legal person who is responsible for the collection, processing and
     recovery of reusable materials and who applies a decontamination process.
     e. Market surveillance authority: an authority designated by a Member State as responsible for
     carrying out market surveillance in the territory of that Member State.
3. Customs authorities: government service which is responsible for the administration of customs law
and the collection of duties and taxes and which also has the responsibility for the application of other
laws and regulations relating to the importation, exportation, movement or storage of goods.
4. Service provider: any natural or legal person providing an information service.
     a. DPP service provider (main or back-up): a natural or legal person that is an independent third-

        1. Main DPP service provider as "digital product passport service provider (EN 18221:2026, 3.3) that

5 Requirements for the protection of business confidentiality and the
management of access rights / 商业保密保护与访问权限管理要求
5.1 General / 概述
Based on the product life cycle and the different business roles along this life cycle (4.2) the following
requirements for DPP access rights management, information system security and business
confidentiality hold.
  基于产品生命周期及该生命周期中的不同业务角色（4.2），以下 DPP 访问权限管理、信息系统安全和商业保密
  要求适用。
5.2 Requirements for the protection of business confidentiality / 商业保密保护要求
1. Actors in a DPP system (see 4.2) shall have globally unique operator identifiers, as defined in EN
18219:2026.
2. Access to public data shall be possible without authentication.
   NOTE 1 This requirement avoids discrimination based on, inter alia, geographical location.
3. Access and management of controlled DPP data shall not be prohibited based solely on an actor's
location or jurisdiction.
4. Actors shall be able to obtain and provide (when requested) all the information needed for
identification, authorization and accounting purposes, including the unique operator identifier.
   NOTE 2 This requirement avoids discrimination based on, inter alia, geographical location.
5. Authentication of actors accessing or managing controlled DPP data shall allow for accountability.
Management (including creation, modification and access) of controlled DPP data shall be implemented
using mechanisms for non-repudiation.
6. The EO may assign certain rights and roles to any notified actor (see 4.2) that requires access to its
controlled DPP data based on their globally unique operator identifier. Such access rights shall be product
group specific and conform to the relevant legal act.
7. Roles granting access to regulated DPP data are defined per product group by relevant legal act. The
EOs may grant additional access privileges.
8. Globally unique operator identifier shall be provided as part of the authentication process. For example,
the globally unique operator identifier may be part of a trustworthy role credential, allowing for access to
controlled DPP data without the need of individual EO's explicit approval.
9. For actors fulfilling additional roles not regulated but defined by the EO, the EO can decide which
authentication mechanism to apply.
10. Access rights terms shall be defined at data element level. Controlled vocabularies, for example the EU

6 Requirements on system and service resilience / 系统与服务韧性要求
6.1 General requirements / 通用要求
There shall be no authentication to read access of public DPP data. The authentication functionality
should be simple, reliable and secured. To access and amend controlled data, the authentication is
required.
The authentication process, where required, shall apply to both actors and system components.
  对公共 DPP 数据的读取访问不应要求认证。认证功能应简单、可靠且安全。访问和修改受控数据需要认证。
  在需要认证的情况下，认证过程应同时适用于主体和系统组件。
6.2 Access management / 访问管理
From the functional requirements specified in Clause 5 the following requirements for access
management arise.
EOs control the access to DPP data of products they are liable for. Access schemes to DPP data can be
separated into:
1. Unauthenticated access to public DPP data: read-only access to publicly available DPP information by
any actor.
2. Authenticated access to controlled DPP data: Role based access to controlled DPP data; e.g. access by
authorities, access by parties with roles defined by regulation. Different roles will permit different
operations on specific sets of data.
  由第 5 条规定的功能要求产生以下访问管理要求。
  EO 控制其负责的产品的 DPP 数据访问。DPP 数据的访问方案可分为：
  1. 对公共 DPP 数据的未认证访问：任何主体对公开可用 DPP 信息的只读访问。
  2. 对受控 DPP 数据的认证访问：基于角色的受控 DPP 数据访问；例如，主管部门的访问、具有法规定义角色
  的各方的访问。不同角色将允许对特定数据集执行不同的操作。
6.3 Access revoking policy / 访问撤销策略
A policy for "access revocation" shall be put in place for the DPP system and services.
  应为 DPP 系统和服务制定"访问撤销"策略。
6.4 Digital operational resilience / 数字运营韧性
6.4.1 General / 概述
Digital operational resilience aims at the resilience of the overall supply chain of ICT services [6, 8], ICT
systems or ICT products throughout their life cycle against disruptive events.
  数字运营韧性旨在实现 ICT 服务 [6, 8]、ICT 系统或 ICT 产品在整个生命周期中针对破坏性事件的整体供应链韧
  性。

As security is a moving target, continuous improvement (CI) of all security measures and policies is

6.5 Security management / 安全管理
6.5.1 Service availability / 服务可用性
The availability and timely accessibility of digital product data are essential as defined by/in implementing
acts.
  数字产品数据的可用性和及时可访问性至关重要，如实施法案中所定义。
6.5.2 Security by design / 安全设计
Services managing DPP data should be designed and managed following security-by-design and defence
in depth methodologies. A security by design approach should be followed in design, implementation and
maintenance of all the DPP related systems and services. Measures on how to uphold and preserve
reliable, resilient and secure information systems during the whole life cycle of critical DPP data shall be
identified and implemented. Thereby the following aspects shall be assessed and considered:
— overall quality and resilience of products and services;
— cybersecurity risk-management measures embedded in them; and
— cybersecurity practices of their suppliers and service providers, including their secure development
procedures.
Cybersecurity risk-management measures should be included into contractual arrangements with direct
suppliers and service providers.
  管理 DPP 数据的服务应遵循安全设计和纵深防御方法论进行设计和管理。在所有 DPP 相关系统和服务的设
  计、实施和维护中应遵循安全设计方法。应识别并实施在关键 DPP 数据整个生命周期内维护和保持可靠、有
  韧性和安全的信息系统的措施。应评估和考虑以下方面：
  — 产品和服务的整体质量和韧性；
  — 嵌入其中的网络安全风险管理措施；以及
  — 其供应商和服务提供商的网络安全实践，包括其安全开发程序。
网络安全风险管理措施应纳入与直接供应商和服务提供商的合同安排中。

6.5.3 Incident detection and response / 事件检测与响应
Cybersecurity risk-management measures should consider critical data flows and related information
systems and include measures related to the prevention, detection, identification, containment, analysis
and response to incidents.
  网络安全风险管理措施应考虑关键数据流和相关信息系统，并包括与事件预防、检测、识别、遏制、分析和响

6.5.4 Recovery capability / 恢复能力
Critical data flows and related information systems should be equipped with redundant systems and back-
up resources to ensure continuity. There should be resilience mechanisms implemented to mitigate
Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks and allow for the recovery to a stable state in the event of such an attack.
  关键数据流和相关信息系统应配备冗余系统和备份资源以确保连续性。应实施韧性机制以缓解拒绝服务
  （DoS）攻击，并在此类攻击发生时恢复到稳定状态。
6.5.5 Continuous improvement / 持续改进
CI processes for Information Security Management System (ISMS) should be put in place for DPP systems
and services. Such processes may be implemented in accordance with EN ISO/IEC 27001:2023.
The process for establishing and maintaining ISMS should be based on a Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle
to ensure systematic enhancement of information security.
Planning (Plan): The organization should establish objectives and scope for the ISMS. A comprehensive
risk assessment should be conducted to identify potential threats and vulnerabilities. Appropriate security
controls should be selected and documented based on the risk assessment.
Implementation (Do): The organization should implement the security controls defined in the planning
phase. All personnel should be trained and made aware of their roles and responsibilities regarding
information security. Necessary security measures should be deployed, and policies shall be enforced.
Monitoring and review (Check): The effectiveness of the implemented controls should be continuously
monitored and reviewed. Key performance indicators (KPIs) should be measured and analysed to assess
the health of the ISMS.
Improvement (Act): Corrective and preventive actions should be taken based on the results of monitoring
and review. Processes should be proactively enhanced to improve the ISMS. Continuous improvement
should be ensured by addressing identified issues and implementing necessary changes.
   应为 DPP 系统和服务建立信息安全管理体系（ISMS）的持续改进流程。此类流程可根据 EN ISO/IEC
   27001:2023 实施。
   建立和维护 ISMS 的流程应基于计划-执行-检查-改进（PDCA）循环，以确保信息安全的系统性提升。
   策划（Plan）： 组织应确立 ISMS 的目标和范围。应进行全面的风险评估以识别潜在威胁和脆弱性。应根据风
   险评估选择并记录适当的安全控制措施。
   执行（Do）： 组织应实施在策划阶段定义的安全控制措施。所有人员应接受培训并了解其在信息安全方面的角
   色和职责。应部署必要的安全措施，并强制执行政策。
   检查（Check）： 应持续监控和审查已实施控制措施的有效性。应测量和分析关键绩效指标（KPI）以评估
   ISMS 的健康状况。
改进（Act）： 应根据监控和审查的结果采取纠正和预防措施。应主动改进流程以提升 ISMS。应通过解决已识
别的问题并实施必要的变更来确保持续改进。

            Authentication assumptions / 认证假设（资料性）
Authentication to manage controlled DPP data is based on the following assumptions, considering the risk
associated with erroneous authentication:
1. Relevant identity data for identity proofing and verification has been collected.
2. There is a strong confidence in the claimed or asserted identity of the entity being authenticated.
3. Multifactor authentication is employed with at least two different authentication factors.
4. The electronic identification means is designed in such a way that it could be reasonably assumed that,
when it is used, it is under the sole control of the entity (natural or legal person) to whom it belongs.
5. All relevant cryptographic information exchanged in the authentication process is protected while in
transit and at rest.
6. The claimed identity of the entity (natural or legal person) is demonstrated on the basis of evidence
recognized by an authoritative source and it is checked against such source.
7. Steps have been taken to minimize the risk that the evidence of identity was lost, stolen, suspended,
revoked or based on expired documents.
8. The binding between the authentication means and the identity is based on recognized procedures and
it is registered in an authoritative source and has been verified on the basis of information from that
source.
9. The electronic identification means is delivered in a way that provides the reasonable assumption that
it is delivered only into the possession of the person to whom it belongs.
10. Suspension and revocation of the electronic identification means could be done in a timely manner.
11. Personal identification data are released only after a reliable verification of the electronic
identification means has been performed by a dynamic authentication.
   管理受控 DPP 数据的认证基于以下假设，同时考虑错误认证相关的风险：
   1. 已收集用于身份核验和验证的相关身份数据。
   2. 对被认证实体声称或断言的身份具有高度信心。
   3. 采用多因素认证，至少使用两种不同的认证因素。
   4. 电子身份识别手段的设计使得可以合理假设，在使用时，它仅处于其所属实体（自然人或法人）的单独控制
   之下。
   5. 认证过程中交换的所有相关加密信息在传输和静态存储时均受到保护。
   6. 实体（自然人或法人）的声称身份基于权威来源认可的证据予以证明，并与该来源进行核对。
   7. 已采取措施最大限度地降低身份证明丢失、被盗、暂停、撤销或基于过期文件的风险。
   8. 认证手段与身份之间的绑定基于公认的程序，已注册在权威来源中，并已根据该来源的信息进行验证。
   9. 电子身份识别手段的发放方式使得可以合理假设其仅交付给所属之人。
   10. 电子身份识别手段的暂停和撤销可及时进行。
   11. 个人身份数据仅在通过动态认证对电子身份识别手段进行可靠验证后才予释放。
